Energy

What the processor draws under Hypoland and under stock Hyprland 0.57.1, in Omarchy's session, on the 7 machines of the lab whose CPUs count their energy (RAPL): the Samsung Series 7 Ultra (NP730U3E), Apple Mac mini Late 2014, Dell Chromebook 11 3120, Lenovo ThinkPad T550, Dell XPS 13 9343, Lenovo ThinkPad T460s and Lenovo ThinkPad T490. The same workloads under both, each 30 seconds, with the power profile Omarchy picks on battery (balanced). Every cell is Hypoland / Hyprland: green where Hypoland is at least 5% better, red where it is at least 5% worse.

Preliminary: the run is still going, these are its first 1 fresh session of each compositor per machine.

RAPL counts the CPU package: the cores, the integrated GPU and the memory controller, which is where a compositor spends its energy. The screen, the disk and the rest of the machine draw the same under both, so a battery lasts longer by a smaller share than these figures show; the whole machine is in the last table, on the two laptops that report it.

How it was measured

Medians of 1 fresh session of each compositor per machine, alternating on 2026-11-12 from 11:51, Hypoland master r972 (after 0.3.0) against Hyprland 0.57.1-2. Each session started 60 seconds before the first workload and ran every workload once, with the power over the workload read from RAPL's counters twice a second and an idle baseline of 10 seconds before and after it. The machines ran on their chargers (RAPL does not need the battery), with the system's timers and Omarchy's update checks stopped. The workloads are those whose clients do the same work under any compositor: they draw at the screen's rate, at a video's, or by a timer or key repeat. Not the terminals' print loops or the games, which do more work under a compositor that takes their frames faster, so their energy says nothing about the compositor's (their frames are on terminals and games). The X200, the AMD desktop, the Galaxy Book and the Mac mini M1 have no RAPL and are not here. On the Mac mini 2014 the workspace switching is left out: in every session of both compositors its terminals were not on the workspaces it switched between, so it measured almost nothing (5 frames a second instead of about 49 in earlier runs). Not yet looked into. The raw data, every session's values and the scripts go to docs/baseline when the run ends.
