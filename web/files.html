Nautilus, Flea, Folio

Each file manager opens a folder of 5000 empty files in its list view, and the Down key is held for 12 seconds on a virtual keyboard: the compositor passes on the one press and the file manager repeats it, about 480 files. On every machine of the lab, in Omarchy's session, under Hypoland and under stock Hyprland 0.57.2. Every cell is Hypoland / Hyprland: green where Hypoland is at least 5% better, red where it is at least 5% worse; a dash where the file manager does not run or there is nothing to count.

File managerVersionWhat it is
Nautilus51.1GNOME's file manager, GTK 4: drawn with Vulkan or OpenGL into GPU buffers, on the X200 with cairo into shared memory.
Flea0.4.2A keyboard-first file manager for Omarchy, written in Rust with GPUI (the Zed editor's UI toolkit) and drawn on the GPU through wgpu.
Folio2e2a716 (after 0.5.0)Omarchy's own file manager, written in C++ with Qt Quick.

Flea and Folio: medians of 9 to 16 fresh sessions of Hypoland and 9 to 16 of Hyprland per machine, Hypoland master r1029 (after 0.3.0) against Hyprland 0.57.2-2, alternating through the night of 2026-11-17 to 18. The two alternated, a fresh session each, so a drift of the machine hits both the same. The Samsung Galaxy Book4 Edge and the Mac mini Late 2014 were not in that night: theirs are from the night of 2026-11-14 to 15, Hypoland master r992 against the same Hyprland 0.57.2-2. Nautilus: medians of 13 to 17 fresh sessions of each compositor per machine, the night of 2026-11-11 to 12, alternating Hypoland master r966 with Hyprland 0.57.1-2, the last night with Nautilus on the machines (Omarchy's update of 2026-11-13 removed it); HP Notebook (A6-7310) and Lenovo ThinkPad P14s Gen 2a joined after it, and the Mac mini 2014 ran on a headless output then. Folio is commit 2e2a716 (folio#79, after 0.5.0), built on every machine, which scrolls the list to the cursor on Down. The Galaxy Book's and the Mac mini 2014's night had Omarchy's Folio 0.4.0, which does not (folio#18): the cursor walks off the bottom and the view stays, so their Folio scrolling cells measure the cursor and the details pane redrawn. Flea opens no window on the X200 (it needs more than OpenGL 2.1) and is not installed on the Mac mini M1. Under Hyprland Nautilus opened no window on the X200 in its night. On the X200 Hyprland runs with Omarchy's GLES 3 override (omarchy#14764), which the file managers get too. The Mac mini M1's display driver reports no page flips, so it has no frames on the screen. Raw data, every session's values and the scripts: 20261117-all-machines-r1029-vs-hyprland-0.57.2, and for those two 20261114-all-machines-r992-vs-hyprland-0.57.2. Nautilus's night: 20261111-all-machines-r966-vs-hyprland-0.57.1.
