Games and 3D

Games and 3D benchmarks, fullscreen and without vsync, in Omarchy's session on every machine of the lab, under Hypoland and under stock Hyprland 0.57.2. Every cell is Hypoland / Hyprland: green where Hypoland is at least 5% better, red where it is at least 5% worse; a dash where the benchmark does not run or there is nothing to count. The game's frame rate is what the game drew; the frames on the screen are the compositor's page flips a second, which stop at the screen's refresh rate (120 Hz on the Galaxy Book).

From the night of the lab page: Hypoland 0.4.0 against Hyprland 0.57.2-2, alternating through the night of 2026-11-24. The X200 and the Turbo-X hung in a Hyprland session that night and add the sessions of 11-25. A fullscreen game under Hypoland can go straight to the screen (direct scanout): Hypoland measures each fullscreen window both ways and keeps the better. The Mac mini M1's display driver reports no page flips. On the X200 and the Turbo-X Hyprland runs with Omarchy's GLES 3 override (omarchy#14764), which the games get too. Raw data: 20261124-all-machines-0.4.0-vs-hyprland-0.57.2, 20261125-x200-turbox-0.4.0-vs-hyprland-0.57.2.
