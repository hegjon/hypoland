Games and 3D

Games and 3D benchmarks, fullscreen and without vsync, in Omarchy's session on every machine of the lab, under Hypoland and under stock Hyprland 0.57.1. Every cell is Hypoland / Hyprland: green where Hypoland is at least 5% better, red where it is at least 5% worse; a dash where the benchmark does not run or there is nothing to count. The game's frame rate is what the game drew; the frames on the screen are the compositor's page flips a second, which stop at the screen's refresh rate (120 Hz on the Galaxy Book).

Xonotic, GravityMark and ElectroBench are from the night of the lab page, alternating from 2026-11-10 23:09 to 11-11 07:30, Hypoland master r957 (after 0.3.0) against Hyprland 0.57.1-2; SuperTuxKart, Yamagi Quake II and vkmark came to the benchmark after it and are from a short run, 2 fresh sessions of each compositor per machine (Hypoland 3 on some), in the afternoon of 2026-11-11, Hypoland r964 (the compositor of r957 with two small fixes) against Hyprland 0.57.1-2: few sessions, so read those tables as a first look. A fullscreen game under Hypoland can go straight to the screen (direct scanout): Hypoland measures each fullscreen window both ways and keeps the better. The Mac mini 2014 had no monitor for either run (it has a headless output in the short run, so no frames on the screen); the Mac mini M1's display driver reports no page flips. On the X200 Hyprland runs with Omarchy's GLES 3 override (omarchy#14764), which the games get too. Raw data, every session's values and the scripts: the night, the short run.
