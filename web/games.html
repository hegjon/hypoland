Games and 3D

Games and 3D benchmarks, fullscreen and without vsync, in Omarchy's session on every machine of the lab, under Hypoland and under stock Hyprland 0.57.2. Every cell is Hypoland / Hyprland: green where Hypoland is at least 5% better, red where it is at least 5% worse; a dash where the benchmark does not run or there is nothing to count. The game's frame rate is what the game drew; the frames on the screen are the compositor's page flips a second, which stop at the screen's refresh rate (120 Hz on the Galaxy Book).

From the night of the lab page: Hypoland master r1029 (after 0.3.0) against Hyprland 0.57.2-2, alternating through the night of 2026-11-17 to 18. The two alternated, a fresh session each, so a drift of the machine hits both the same. The Samsung Galaxy Book4 Edge and the Mac mini Late 2014 were not in that night: theirs are from the night of 2026-11-14 to 15, Hypoland master r992 against the same Hyprland 0.57.2-2. A fullscreen game under Hypoland can go straight to the screen (direct scanout): Hypoland measures each fullscreen window both ways and keeps the better. The Mac mini M1's display driver reports no page flips. On the X200 Hyprland runs with Omarchy's GLES 3 override (omarchy#14764), which the games get too. Raw data, every session's values and the scripts: 20261117-all-machines-r1029-vs-hyprland-0.57.2, and for those two 20261114-all-machines-r992-vs-hyprland-0.57.2.
