foot, Alacritty, Ghostty, kitty

Each terminal runs while true; do ls -la /usr/lib; done in a tiled window of Omarchy's session and scrolls as fast as it can for 12 seconds, on every machine of the lab, under Hypoland and under stock Hyprland 0.57.2. Every cell is Hypoland / Hyprland: green where Hypoland is at least 5% better, red where it is at least 5% worse; a dash where the terminal does not run.

TerminalVersionHow it draws
foot1.28.0On the CPU, into shared memory (wl_shm): the compositor makes the texture, by zero copy where it can.
Alacritty0.17.0OpenGL ES 2 / OpenGL 3.3, GPU buffers (dmabuf); runs on the X200 too.
Ghostty1.4.0A GTK 4 window drawn by Ghostty's own OpenGL 3.3 renderer, GPU buffers.
kitty0.50.0OpenGL 3.3, GPU buffers.

Medians of 9 to 16 fresh sessions of Hypoland and 9 to 16 of Hyprland per machine, Hypoland master r1029 (after 0.3.0) against Hyprland 0.57.2-2, alternating through the night of 2026-11-17 to 18. The two alternated, a fresh session each, so a drift of the machine hits both the same. The Samsung Galaxy Book4 Edge and the Mac mini Late 2014 were not in that night: theirs are from the night of 2026-11-14 to 15, Hypoland master r992 against the same Hyprland 0.57.2-2. The Mac mini M1's display driver reports no page flips, so it has no frames on the screen. Ghostty and kitty need OpenGL 3.3: not on the X200, and Ghostty does not start on the Chromebook and the Samsung (Bay Trail and Ivy Bridge). On the X200 Hyprland runs with Omarchy's GLES 3 override (omarchy#14764), which the terminals get too. Raw data, every session's values and the scripts: 20261117-all-machines-r1029-vs-hyprland-0.57.2, and for those two 20261114-all-machines-r992-vs-hyprland-0.57.2.
