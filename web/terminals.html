foot, Alacritty, Ghostty, kitty

Each terminal runs while true; do ls -la /usr/lib; done in a tiled window of Omarchy's session and scrolls as fast as it can for 12 seconds, on every machine of the lab, under Hypoland and under stock Hyprland 0.57.2. Every cell is Hypoland / Hyprland: green where Hypoland is at least 5% better, red where it is at least 5% worse; a dash where the terminal does not run.

TerminalVersionHow it draws
foot1.28.0On the CPU, into shared memory (wl_shm): the compositor makes the texture, by zero copy where it can.
Alacritty0.17.0OpenGL ES 2 / OpenGL 3.3, GPU buffers (dmabuf); runs on the X200 too.
Ghostty1.4.0A GTK 4 window drawn by Ghostty's own OpenGL 3.3 renderer, GPU buffers.
kitty0.50.0OpenGL 3.3, GPU buffers.

Medians of 5 to 16 fresh sessions of Hypoland and 5 to 16 of Hyprland per machine, Hypoland master r1030 (after 0.3.0) as a PGO build against Hyprland 0.57.2-2, alternating through the night of 2026-11-18 to 19; released packages are plain builds, and those measured 4 to 6 % more compositor CPU. The two alternated, a fresh session each, so a drift of the machine hits both the same. The Samsung Galaxy Book4 Edge and the Mac mini Late 2014 were not in that night: theirs are from the night of 2026-11-14 to 15, Hypoland master r992 against the same Hyprland 0.57.2-2. The ThinkPad P14s and the Mac mini M1 are from the night of 2026-11-17 to 18 (Hypoland master r1029, a plain build): the P14s's PGO sessions aborted in the video workload in every one of them, and the M1's whole night moved, its stock Hyprland at 1.19 times the CPU and 1.40 the latencies it measured on 11-17, so neither machine's PGO pair is shown. The ThinkPad X200 and the Turbo-X are from the Hypoland 0.4.0 release, a plain build, against the same Hyprland 0.57.2-2, alternating through 2026-11-24 to 25: 5 fresh sessions of each on the X200 so far, 12 on the Turbo-X. The Mac mini M1's display driver reports no page flips, so it has no frames on the screen. Ghostty and kitty need OpenGL 3.3: not on the X200 and the Turbo-X, and Ghostty does not start on the Chromebook and the Samsung (Bay Trail and Ivy Bridge). On the X200 and the Turbo-X Hyprland runs with Omarchy's GLES 3 override (omarchy#14764), which the terminals get too. Raw data, every session's values and the scripts: 20261118-all-machines-r1030-pgo-vs-hyprland-0.57.2, for the P14s and the Mac mini M1 20261117-all-machines-r1029-vs-hyprland-0.57.2, for the Galaxy Book and the Mac mini 2014 20261114-all-machines-r992-vs-hyprland-0.57.2, and for the X200 and the Turbo-X 20261125-x200-turbox-0.4.0-vs-hyprland-0.57.2.
